# Enrollment Recovery — Requirements

Recover students who abandon enrollment mid-checkout by sending a
same-day nudge sequence and flagging repeat drop-offs to the advising team.

## Trigger

Checkout session abandoned for more than 2 hours with no completed payment.

## Success criteria

- Nudge sequence sent within 1 business hour of abandonment.
- Repeat abandoners (2+ in 30 days) flagged to advising.
