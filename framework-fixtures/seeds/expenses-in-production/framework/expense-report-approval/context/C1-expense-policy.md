# Expense Policy v7 (text copy)

- Hotel: up to $250 a night, room and tax only. Minibar, movies and laundry are not reimbursed.
- Meals: up to $75 a day while travelling, including tip; a tip over 20% is not reimbursed.
- Taxi and ride share: up to $60 a trip.
- Every line needs an itemised receipt. A line with no receipt, or a receipt with no line, is returned.
- Foreign-currency receipts are converted at the rate on the receipt date.
- A report over $5,000 needs the controller's approval before it is paid.
