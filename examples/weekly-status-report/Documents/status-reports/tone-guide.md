# Tone guide — Maya's status reports

- Direct. Say what happened and what it means; no preamble.
- No hedging. "Appears to", "may have", "seems" are banned — state it or leave it out.
- Lead with what changed this week, not with background.
- Every blocker names its owner and the next action. "Owner needed" when there is none.
- Plain language for leadership: no task IDs, no tracker jargon, no acronyms without a gloss.
- Under 400 words. Four sections in this order: Wins, In Progress, Blockers, Next Week.
