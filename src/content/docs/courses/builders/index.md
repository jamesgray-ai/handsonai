**You already use Claude. Now make it accelerate your business.**

Most people use Claude for one-off prompts, and each one starts from a blank chat. The next step is building AI workflows that work with you or without you, so you scale what already works and innovate what's next. In this five-week cohort you build in every session, on your own work.

[Enroll on Maven →](https://maven.com/james-gray/claude)

## Who This Course Is For

- **Team or discipline leaders** scaling their team's output with Claude, turning expertise into AI workflows the whole team reuses
- **Founders and entrepreneurs** who prototype fast, automate operations, and launch new ideas on Claude without waiting on engineers
- **AI-first professionals** who use Claude daily and want AI workflows at the center of how they create value

## What You'll Do

- Design agentic AI solutions from the workflows your business already runs
- Connect Claude to your tools and a knowledge graph that grows as you work
- Build skill-powered workflows that produce your repeatable deliverables
- Build single-agent and multi-agent systems for your highest-value work
- Own your skills and agents in GitHub, portable across platforms and shipped to your team as a plugin
- Keep an AI Registry: one record of every workflow and the AI behind it
