Google replaced **Gemini CLI** with **Antigravity CLI** (the `agy` command) for free and Google AI Pro / Ultra users on June 18, 2026.

**→ [Antigravity CLI](../antigravity-cli/)**: install, sign in, add skills, and move over from Gemini CLI.

Gemini CLI still exists for organizations on Gemini Code Assist Standard or Enterprise: [Gemini CLI on GitHub](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli).
