The framework now uses one rule for the autonomy level, and the [Analyze](/ai-workflow-framework/analyze/) and [Design](/ai-workflow-framework/design/) skills apply it the way the [AI Workflow Design Matrix](/ai-workflow-framework/workflow-design-matrix/) teaches it: look at **what decides the next step**. Deterministic means you give instructions, and the AI's output never changes what happens next, even when it writes inside a step. Guided means you give bounded decisions, with your method: the AI uses your rubric or criteria to route an item, choose a tool, or grade work and send it back. Autonomous means you give a goal and the AI plans its own steps.

<!-- more -->

Involvement is now about taking part, not the trigger: a workflow is Augmented when a person is in it along the way, and starting a run by hand no longer counts. Design gained reference cases to calibrate against, and the sample Analyze reports and example pages were reclassified under the rule. Also in 8.1.2: the registry setup skill says "the Claude desktop app" instead of Cowork.
