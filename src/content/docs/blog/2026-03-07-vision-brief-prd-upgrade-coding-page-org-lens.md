## Vision Brief & Feature PRD skills — deeper PM rigor

The [writing-vision-briefs](../../use-cases/coding/agentic-coding.mdx) and [writing-feature-prds](../../use-cases/coding/agentic-coding.mdx) skills in the agentic-coding plugin (v1.2.4) received a significant upgrade. Vision Briefs now include a **go/no-go decision gate** that assesses problem severity, strategic alignment, and confidence before you invest in scoping — with options to proceed, validate first, or park the idea. New discovery questions cover **stakeholders**, **dependencies**, **alternatives considered**, and **problem quantification**. Feature PRDs expanded from 5 to 10 discovery questions and added sections for **non-functional requirements**, **error states**, **success metrics & instrumentation**, **migration & rollback**, and **UI/UX requirements**. Acceptance criteria now use **MoSCoW priority tags** (`[MUST]`/`[SHOULD]`/`[COULD]`), and a detailed Vision→PRD mapping table ensures nothing gets lost in the handoff.

## Agentic Coding page moves to Use Cases > Coding

The [Agentic Coding](../../use-cases/coding/agentic-coding.mdx) page moved from the plugin catalog to **Use Cases > Coding**, where it belongs as a coding methodology rather than a plugin listing. The page was restructured to lead with the 7-step feature development lifecycle before diving into component details and installation instructions.

## Business-First AI Framework adds organizational lens

The [Business-First AI Framework](../../business-first-ai-framework/index.md) skills now support two workflow lenses: **individual** (personal productivity workflows) and **organizational** (value chain workflows tied to business objectives). The analyzing, deconstructing, and building skills adapt their questions, outputs, and scoping based on which lens you select — adding stakeholder tracking, role swimlanes, and change management guidance for organizational workflows.
