Two changes to the start of the framework, from a live cohort. [Analyze](/ai-workflow-framework/analyze/) now stays at the strategic level — it names the work, the pain, the kind of value AI adds, and what gets produced, never the tool or connector — and when you have a registry it walks your processes asking where the friction is instead of running a generic question bank. The [registry interview](/builder-setup/ai-registry-setup/) asks who it is for (a business you own, a company you lead, your role, or your personal life), reads what you already have — an org chart, a services page, a strategy doc — and drafts your nodes for you to confirm. It ends by offering Analyze straight away.

<!-- more -->

- **Analyze**: "Getting started" on each card is replaced by a **value lever** (Streamline, Automate, Accelerate, Create value) and *what changes for the business*. Connected tools are ignored during discovery.
- **Registry**: Phase 4 now asks for the processes that deliver each line of business, ranked by importance — the map, not a shortlist. Your top objectives live in your Business node so no step asks for them twice.
- **Deconstruct**: arrives pre-loaded with your objectives and the candidate's value lever, and its five signposts now say "Part", matching the course pages.
