Content creation use cases have AI draft, edit, translate, reformat, and repurpose written and visual content — handling the production work so you can focus on message, strategy, and quality control. The deliverable is something people read, watch, or interact with: emails, reports, blog posts, social posts, presentations, documentation.

Quality scales with the context you provide — brand voice docs, style guides, examples, and audience profiles turn generic output into something that actually sounds like you.

*Content Creation is one of six use case primitives identified in OpenAI's [Identifying and Scaling AI Use Cases](../../resources/openai-use-cases-report/), adapted here to be platform-agnostic.*

## When to apply this primitive

**Use Content Creation when:**

- The primary deliverable is a written document, message, or media asset
- You're producing similar content repeatedly (weekly reports, social posts, email templates)
- You need to adapt content for different audiences, formats, or languages
- The task involves editing, proofreading, or reformatting existing content

**Not the right primitive when:**

- The main goal is gathering and synthesizing information — that's [Research](../research/)
- You're generating code, scripts, or technical artifacts — that's [Coding](../coding/)
- The content is a plan, strategy, or set of recommendations — that's [Ideation & Strategy](../ideation-and-strategy/)
