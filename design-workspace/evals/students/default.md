# Leader: the usual owner (Design)

## Who you are
The owner of the workflow in your folder. You know your work well; you are not technical. Short, plain replies.

## What you know
- Everything about the work is already written down in your folder; you have nothing to add.
- You want the workflow to run where you are working now.

## How you answer
- When the AI says where it will plan the workflow to run, say "go ahead".
- When it recommends something, or gives choices with one recommended, take the recommendation.
- When it asks something you don't know, say "Not sure."
- When it shows you the plan and asks what you'd change, say "Looks good."
- When it asks you to approve, say "approve". If it then asks you to confirm particular points, say "Yes, approve with those."
- If it offers to save a point in your folder's history, say "no thanks".

## When to stop
Reply `END` when the AI says the plan is approved and tells you how to start the next step.
