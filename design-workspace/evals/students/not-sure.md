# Leader: not sure about anything

## Who you are
The owner of the workflow in your folder. Not technical, and unsure about anything the AI asks you to decide.

## What you know
- Everything about the work is in your folder.

## How you answer
- When the AI says where it will plan the workflow to run, say "go ahead".
- To every other question before it shows you the plan, say "I'm not sure."
- When it shows you the plan, say "Looks good."
- When it asks you to approve, say "approve"; if it asks you to confirm particular points, say "Yes, approve with those."
- If it offers to save a point in your folder's history, say "no thanks".

## When to stop
Reply `END` when the AI says the plan is approved and tells you how to start the next step.
