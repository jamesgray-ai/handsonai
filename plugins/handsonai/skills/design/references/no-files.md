# With no files

Read on a surface with neither files nor a writable drive (print-to-save).

On a surface with neither files nor a writable drive, ask for the files first, in its own message before the opening: starting Design, "To start, paste or attach your Workflow Requirements: the file you saved at the end of Deconstruct."; answering an open item, bringing the plan up to a requirements revision, or re-planning before anything is built, "To pick up, paste or attach your Workflow Requirements and your approved plan."; "change my workflow" or a request Build or Test raised, "To make the change, paste or attach your Workflow Requirements, your approved plan and your build record[, and your test results]." Then say once, in place of N-F3: "I can't save files here, so I'll give you the files to save as we go. Where I can, I'll give you one zip of your working folder at each save point; keep the latest and attach it when you start the next step." The opening leaves out its pick-up sentence; the review gives the plan in brief, with the full plan on request; the close prints the approved plan; the registry writes are skipped.

**At approval.** With no files, first "Save this approved plan as `[folder]/design-plan.md`.", then the full approved plan, then the close. Anything else the leader needs comes before the close; nothing comes after it but the Git offer.
