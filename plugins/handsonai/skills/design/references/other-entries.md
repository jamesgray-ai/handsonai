# Entries that aren't a fresh start, a resume or a change

Read when the routing state is 1 and Design owns it, or when state 4 has no plan but a build record or test results still sit beside a `history/set-aside-*/` folder. Section names in italics are SKILL.md's.

## An interrupted set-aside

| Entry | What Design does |
|---|---|
| State 4, no plan, with a build record or results (`test-results.md`, or a `history/test-results-*.md` at the top of `history/`) still beside a `history/set-aside-*/` folder (an interrupted platform or mechanism change) | Move what's left into the newest set-aside folder, remove the node's "Design plan" and "Test results" links where still there, say so in one line, then the full flow |

## An earlier version Design owns

1. **An earlier version Design owns** (`routing-rare.md`, *Earlier versions*: a `design-spec.md`, or a `design-plan.md` with no `requirements_revision:`): when the workflow is live or was built, N-B44 first. Move that file and every later file in the chain (the build record, `test-results.md` and every `history/test-results-*.md` at the top of `history/` (the finished rounds included)) into `history/earlier-version-YYYY-MM-DD/`, say so in one line (after N-B44, its first sentence already did), and begin as for a new workflow. Nothing is read, converted or played back from the old plan.
