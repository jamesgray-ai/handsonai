# Brand Guidelines: Northwind Advisory (fictional example)

This file stands in for a skill the user already has installed, called `applying-northwind-brand`. It is invented for testing and describes no real company.

**Description:** Applies Northwind Advisory's voice, tone and formatting to anything written for clients or the public: emails, proposals, social posts, website copy. Use when drafting or editing external content for Northwind. Not for internal notes.

## Voice

- Plain, confident and specific. Lead with the point, then the reason.
- First person plural ("we"), addressing the reader as "you".
- No hype words: never "game-changing", "revolutionary", "unlock".
- Short paragraphs: three sentences at most.

## Social posts (LinkedIn)

- Open with a concrete observation from client work, not a question.
- One idea per post; 120–200 words.
- End with a specific question to the reader, never "Thoughts?".
- At most two hashtags, at the end.

## Formatting

- Sentence case for headings.
- Numbers as digits from 10 up.
- Product and company names spelled exactly as the owner spells them.
