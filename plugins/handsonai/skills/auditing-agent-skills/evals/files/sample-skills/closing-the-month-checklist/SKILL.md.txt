# Closing-the-month checklist

1. Read the finance calendar for this month's close dates.
2. Carry over any open items from last month's checklist.
3. List the tasks with owners and due dates, in the order the close needs them.

## Gotchas
- Due dates fall on working days only.

The test cases for this skill are in `evals/evals.json`.
