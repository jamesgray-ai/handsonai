# With no files

Read on a surface with neither files nor a drive that can write (print-to-save), before the opening.

On a surface with neither files nor a drive that can write (print-to-save), first ask for your Workflow Requirements, your approved plan, your build record, your last test results, and the practice inputs and context files saved with them (paste or attach, or the latest zip: unpack it if you can, `references/saving.md` rule 5), and say once, in place of N-F3 (N-F6): "I can't save files here, so I'll give you the files to save as we go. Where I can, I'll give you one zip of your working folder at each save point; keep the latest and attach it when you start the next step." Give each changed file, complete, at the end of each part, at a pause and at the close; at the round's close that includes every input you wrote or kept, under its `inputs/` name, and the edited text of a kept direct edit under its `history/` name.
