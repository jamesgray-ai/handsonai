## What you know
- **The workflow** (when asked to describe it): "After each training session I run, I write a short recap for the people who attended: what we covered, the three key points, and the links. I start it when the session ends and paste in my notes."
- **Anything written down:** no.
- **Name and description:** accept the AI's recommendation if it's close to "Training Session Recap".
- **What it sends or changes:** "Nothing. It gives me the recap and I send it myself."
- **Taking part:** "Yes, I read it before I send it."
- **Law or regulation:** "No."
- **What it's for:** "So attendees remember the key points and can find the links later."
- **A real past recap:** yes. When asked, paste (made up): "Thanks for coming to 'Safe Loading Basics'. We covered: weight limits, strapping, and the pre-departure check. Key points: never exceed the plate weight; check straps twice; sign the sheet. Links: loading guide, checklist."
- **What you started from** (only when asked): "My notes from the session."
- **What a good one looks like:** "Under 150 words, three key points, every link I mentioned."
- **What you'd check:** three key points; every link in my notes; under 150 words. Send back: a link missing.
- **Sources:** "Just my session notes, pasted in."
- **Rules:** "Never add anything I didn't cover."
- **Practice runs:** keep the AI's suggestions. When asked to paste a real input, paste: "Notes: 'Forklift Refresher' — battery checks, blind spots, speed in the yard. Links: forklift guide, yard map."
- **Defaults message:** "looks fine".
- **Preview:** "go ahead".
- **On screen:** do it and say "done".
